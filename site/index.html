Micrantha / applied research

Digital content, verifiable by design.

What if a digital purchase could be as durable and portable as the physical media it replaced?

CONTENTPKG explores a shared foundation for verifiable digital content packages. Its first specialization, MUSICPKG, is an open format for durable music ownership, designed to separate possession of a file from the rights to use it.

01 / The foundation

Portable packages. Verifiable claims.

The goal is to make content protection and verification reusable without imposing a single business model on every kind of media.

Integrity

Verify that authenticated content and its associated evidence have not been silently modified.

Authenticity

Use cryptographic evidence to establish the provenance of a package under its profile's trust assumptions.

Independent rights profiles

Keep the mechanics of packaging and cryptography separate from ownership, access and transfer rules.

CONTENTPKG is an experimental engineering foundation, not a published general-purpose standard or a commercial rights service.

02 / First specialization

Make digital music like CDs again.

Buy it. Own it. Play it. Back it up. Sell or gift it where permitted.

MUSICPKG proposes a model in which a one-time purchase does not depend on a retailer's continued operation or periodic license renewal.

For listeners

Durable ownership

Play offline after legitimate enrollment, authorize your own devices and recover purchases on replacement hardware.

For creators

Authentic issuance

Sell a legitimate copy once, with cryptographically verifiable issuance and evidence of ownership without remaining in the ordinary playback path.

For collectors

Transferable title

Where a title permits it, the design supports legitimate resale or gifting with a verifiable change of canonical ownership.

For both sides

Copying is not owning

Copying an encrypted package alone is not intended to confer playback authority. Encrypted backups need not create new ownership rights.

A realistic security boundary. MUSICPKG does not promise to prevent all audio capture or erase previously obtained plaintext or keys after a transfer. Canonical title transfer depends on the stated registry trust and finality assumptions.

03 / The opportunity

A different model for digital distribution.

Investors and partners

Explore the intersection of durable digital purchases, direct-to-fan distribution and interoperable ownership evidence. Potential applications include independent storefronts, digital collections and transferable catalogs, subject to rights agreements and technical validation.

The opportunity is a research hypothesis, not a claim of market traction or an investment offering.

Connect with the project author

Artists, labels and publishers

Could a one-time digital sale offer listeners a durable purchase while making casual redistribution of encrypted files less useful? MUSICPKG explores that trade-off without requiring publishers to authorize every ordinary playback.

04 / Engineering and research

Open contracts. Independent implementations.

The published MUSICPKG working draft is intended to let independent implementers build interoperable producers, verifiers and players without a private repository or Micrantha-operated service.

Applied cryptography

Authenticated media, signed issuance, owner-controlled recovery and explicit trust boundaries.

Protocol specifications

Interoperability

Deterministic schemas, test vectors and a public Rust reference conformance harness.

Public test vectors

Security and correctness

Separate historical evidence from current ownership, and state what protection can and cannot guarantee.

Security model
Research status. MUSICPKG v0.2 is a working draft. The public corpus contains 26 executable reference checks and 23 contract assertions, not a complete product-verification suite. Full end-to-end production verification and ownership infrastructure are not established.
