BOOKPKG / future research concept

A book you can keep, not just access.

Imagine buying a digital edition of Entanglement of Ages.

Instead of just opening an ebook in a retailer’s app, imagine owning a complete literary edition: the book itself, its cover, illustrations, the author’s notes and companion material.

For a multi-book series, a collected edition could bring the volumes and their extras together, like a digital boxed set.

Illustrative use case, not an available BOOKPKG edition or published specification.

01 / The reading experience

More than a file.

For readers

Imagine keeping a purchased edition, reading it offline on compatible devices, backing it up and revisiting its companion material without relying on a particular bookstore.

For authors and publishers

Offer a thoughtfully curated premium digital edition, with room for extras and a verifiable connection to the original publication.

Could you gift a book to a friend, sell it or pass it back, as you would a physical book? BOOKPKG is an opportunity to research what legitimate digital transfer might look like when the rights allow it.

02 / Research direction

The same question, a different medium.

Can CONTENTPKG’s ideas about encrypted packaging, authenticity and ownership evidence support literary works as well as music? A book profile would need its own formats, reading experience, accessibility, rights and transfer rules.

Concept only. BOOKPKG is not a published protocol, a finished reader, or a claim that Entanglement of Ages has been packaged or released in this format. No private creative material or implementation is being published.
