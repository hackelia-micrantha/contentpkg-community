MUSICPKG / first specialization

Make digital music like CDs again.

An album is more than its most popular songs.

Imagine buying a digital album from an artist you love. You get every album track and the artist’s B-sides, demos and alternate recordings, not just the songs you heard on a playlist.

Open the digital album sleeve: photographs, artwork, lyrics, liner notes, credits and stories about how the album was made. It could even contain videos, interviews and links to related work.

It is a complete artistic experience you can collect, revisit and keep.

01 / Imagine owning it

Pass the album on, just like a record.

You buy the album and enjoy it. Later, you sell it to a friend or give it to them. They become its owner and can enjoy the same complete edition. If they want to give it back, they transfer ownership back to you.

You buy the album

Pay the artist or seller once for the complete edition.

Your friend owns it

Resell or gift the album through a legitimate transfer.

You get it back

Your friend can return it through a new legitimate transfer.

Where rights and title policy permit transfer, the proposed model has one canonical owner at a time. New transfers require ledger finality; legitimate offline playback is a design goal. Copying the encrypted file alone does not confer ownership.

02 / Why it matters

Bring the value back to the album.

Artists

Instead of relying only on individual tracks and streams, sell a complete creative work at a premium album price. That creates an opportunity to earn more per purchase and reward the effort behind the whole album.

Listeners

Get more than audio files: the artwork, the context, the stories and a meaningful collection to own. Support artists you care about and discover music beyond the hits.

Musicians

Listen to the less popular songs, B-sides and experiments. Hear how an artist develops ideas, tries different arrangements and finds their sound. Those discoveries can inspire your own work.

Streaming can remain a way to discover music. The premium album is something to collect. Artist earnings depend on pricing, rights, costs and distribution; increased income is an opportunity, not a guarantee.
